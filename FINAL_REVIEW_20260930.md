# Final all-15 review, 30 September 2026

The returned desk reports and a fresh correctness/bibliography pass have been
incorporated into all fifteen private review packages. The result is a checked
author-reading set, not publication authorization, a guarantee of correctness
or an external peer-review verdict.

## Material corrections

### Phase-erased entropy production (EP)

The locality statement now has a complete argument for its stated finite-state
class: a fixed connected undirected graph, smooth periodic rates uniformly
bounded above and below on its edges, frozen detailed balance, and a fixed
nonempty reversal-closed set of observed edges. It includes event-phase
weighting, the killed transition operator, uniform tail control and the
conditional-estimator normalization term. These assumptions are substantive;
the theorem does not settle a general finite-period conjecture.

The distinction between Maier et al.'s proven reversed-protocol bound, their
same-protocol estimator and the separate phase-resolved construction is now
explicit, with journal equation numbers 16-19. Kawai-Parrondo-Van den Broeck is
identified as a Hamiltonian phase-space result, not cited as a proof of the
finite-state theorem.

One historical failure needed a mathematical correction. A rate in the old
trial touches zero quadratically. Its entropy-rate singularity is logarithmic
and integrable; an infinite value returned by quadrature at the zero is not
evidence that the integral diverges. The run still fails the declared
strict-positive-rate domain and contains a vacuous acceptance check. Its
failure is preserved, not converted to a pass.

The numerical algorithms were not changed. Both supplied Python calculations
were rerun successfully; the retained Wolfram output was not rerun. Numerical
agreement is high-precision corroboration, not an interval certificate.

### Lens-space and silver-rank papers

LENS now restricts the small L(7,q) zero example to q=2,3,4,5; specifies the
finite range and repetition of the L(31,7) rank list; and distinguishes the
first-eight-prime adjacent-rank count (1,992 nonintegral rows among 2,000) from
the full 31-prime count. The 22-of-31 observation is explicitly at silver rank
J, not adjacent rank J+1. Odd/unit and prime-congruence domains are explicit.

SILVER states the index ranges needed for the Pell formulas, avoids an
undefined negative-index term multiplied by zero, qualifies nonconstant
S-unit orbits, and distinguishes the reported historical blind test from
individually retained predictions. These are domain and evidence corrections,
not a claim that finite checks prove the open complete classification.

A bounded exact replay covers the published extraction, all 364 listed zero
roots and 6,025 retained class triples. A separate rank-one check covers
48,517 unit pairs below modulus 400. The full historical large-Pell computation
and every historical symbolic derivation were not repeated.

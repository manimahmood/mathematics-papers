dependencies. Labels refer to TeX sources; linked READMEs give supplied
check commands. It is not a collection-wide proof or novelty certificate.

## Common conditions

Checksum verifiers check supplied bytes, not every theorem, priority,
historical timestamp or physical claim. Separate implementations corroborate
only their disclosed scope. No review here is represented as journal peer
review or a guarantee that no future correction will be needed.

B accepted the first 1 October correction batch and the final bounded
accuracy/prose delta. The author approved publication of all fifteen preprints.
SM1's outstanding specialist review and SM4's methods-only scope remain
explicit. See `RELEASE_20261001.md` for the current release state and

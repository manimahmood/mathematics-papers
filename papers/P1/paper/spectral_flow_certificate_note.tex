\section{Exact lens-space spectral-flow certificates}
\label{app:certificates}

This supplement provides exact finite reference data for the rank-one
spectral-flow control used in the main text and tabulates the integer-spin eta
formula through rank eight.  Its purpose is reproducibility: it supplies no
additional physical interpretation of the higher-rank invariants.

\subsection{Rank-one certificate}

Let $p$ be odd, let $\gcd(p,q)=1$, and give
\[
 L(p,q)=S^3/\bigl((z_1,z_2)\sim
 (e^{2\pi i/p}z_1,e^{2\pi iq/p}z_2)\bigr)
\]
the complex quotient orientation.  Write
$q^*\in\{1,\ldots,p-1\}$ for the least positive inverse of $q$ modulo $p$.
For $1\leq n\leq(p-1)/2$, define
\begin{equation}
 R_m(p,q)=\frac1p\sum_{k=1}^{p-1}
 \cos\!\left(\frac{2\pi mk}{p}\right)
 \cot\!\left(\frac{\pi k}{p}\right)
 \cot\!\left(\frac{\pi qk}{p}\right).
 \label{eq:certificate-r}
\end{equation}
Jeffrey's Proposition~5.2 \cite{Jeffrey1992}, in these conventions, gives the
integer spectral flow
\begin{equation}
 I_n=\frac{8q^*n^2}{p}-2+R_0(p,q)-R_{2n}(p,q).
 \label{eq:certificate-flow}
\end{equation}
The same proposition supplies the independent counting congruence
\begin{equation}
 I_n\equiv
 1+2\#\{\,0<j<nq^*:jq\equiv\pm n\pmod p\,\}\pmod4.
 \label{eq:certificate-parity}
\end{equation}
Equivalently, the residue is $1$ when
$\langle q^*n\rangle_p<p/2$ and $3$ when
$\langle q^*n\rangle_p>p/2$.

The rank-one eta identity is
\begin{equation}
 R_0(p,q)=4s(q,p)=-\eta_1(L(p,q)).
 \label{eq:certificate-rank-one}
\end{equation}
Thus \eqref{eq:certificate-flow} is an established rank-one control on the
orientation and normalization used in the main theorem.  It is not a premise
of that theorem.

For exact generation, the cotangent expression is evaluated through the
finite Fourier identity
\begin{equation}
 R_m(p,q)=4\sum_{r=0}^{p-1}
 \left(\!\left(\frac rp\right)\!\right)
 \left(\!\left(\frac{qr+m}{p}\right)\!\right),
 \label{eq:certificate-rademacher}
\end{equation}
where the sawtooth is zero at integers and equals
$x-\lfloor x\rfloor-\tfrac12$ otherwise.  Equation
\eqref{eq:certificate-rademacher} uses rational arithmetic throughout and
avoids numerical reconstruction of \eqref{eq:certificate-r}.

\subsection{Dataset and verification hierarchy}

The accompanying files contain every odd $3\leq p\leq61$, every unit
$q\bmod p$, and every $1\leq n\leq(p-1)/2$, together with the $q=1$ blocks
at $p=67,97,137$.  The resulting $16{,}177$ rows record the exact quantities
\[
 I_n,\quad I_n\bmod4,\quad q^*n^2/p\bmod1,\quad
 R_0,\quad R_{2n},\quad s(q,p),
\]
while each $(p,q)$ block records $\eta_J(L(p,q))$ for $1\leq J\leq8$.

The checks have three distinct strengths.

\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Primary acceptance test: exact row equality.}
A computed crossing count passes only if it equals the certified integer
$I_n$.  A floating implementation instead compares directly with that
integer using a declared tolerance.

\item \textbf{Analytic-assembly diagnostic: integrality.}
The right side of \eqref{eq:certificate-flow} must be integral.  This tests
cancellation among independently evaluated rational terms, but it cannot
detect an erroneous result that is already an integer.  In particular,
integrality alone does not certify a crossing count against missed or
double-counted crossings.

\item \textbf{Convention diagnostic: parity.}
Equation \eqref{eq:certificate-parity} is recomputed by direct enumeration,
independently of the $R_m$ assembly.  A disagreement diagnoses a modulo-four
sign or convention error.  Agreement is weaker than exact equality, since
parity cannot see errors in $4\mathbb Z$.
\end{enumerate}

The release verifier checks the complete JSON schema and coverage, modular
inverses, Dedekind and $R_m$ values, Chern--Simons residues, spectral flows,
parities, all $J=1,\ldots,8$ theorem evaluations, duplicate freedom,
CSV/JSON agreement, and every hash recorded in the manifest.

\subsection{Scope boundary}

Only the $J=1$ spectral-flow control in \eqref{eq:certificate-flow} is
attributed to Jeffrey.  The $J\geq2$ entries are exact evaluations of the
theorem proved in the main text; their inclusion makes that theorem directly
reproducible but does not independently prove it.  They are compact-manifold
spectral invariants, not established higher-spin Chern--Simons, one-loop, or
partition-function phases.  No ghost, determinant, framing, counterterm,
coupling, or physical-background analysis is supplied here.  Orientation must
be fixed before any signed comparison.
